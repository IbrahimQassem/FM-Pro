# Device artwork provenance

Front-facing transparent device artwork downloaded on 2026-09-27 from Mobile FIRST:

- `iphone-17-pro.png`: [Apple iPhone 17 Pro](https://www.webmobilefirst.com/en/mockups/apple-iphone-17-pro-2025/), 389 × 800 pixels.
- `galaxy-s26-ultra.png`: [Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra](https://www.webmobilefirst.com/en/mockups/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-2026/), 385 × 800 pixels.

The source pages permit personal/commercial use, modification and cropping,
without attribution or watermarks. They prohibit resale or redistribution of the
standalone source asset. These files are incorporated into the HudHud design
project; do not distribute them as a standalone mockup pack. Device names and
marks belong to their owners; no endorsement is implied.

These are the free standard-resolution assets, not the paid HD versions. They
are scaled in the full-size store compositions. The source offers higher
resolution licensed versions if sharper hardware edges are needed.

`DEVICES` in `studio.js` records the measured transparent display bounds and
corner radii. Screenshots are fitted proportionally beneath the source artwork;
they are not stretched, and no hand-drawn hardware is substituted.
