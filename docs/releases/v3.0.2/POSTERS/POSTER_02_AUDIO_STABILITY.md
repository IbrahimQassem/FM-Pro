# Poster Concept 02 — Background Playback & Uninterrupted Audio

* **Concept**: Acoustic Soundwave Shield & Continuous Audio
* **Core Slogan**: «استماع مستمر... بلا انقطاع» (Continuous Listening... Uninterrupted)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: Audio waves continuing smoothly through a locked device shield

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A dynamic visual illustrating rock-solid background audio stability. In the center, a locked smartphone sits with its screen dark except for the persistent media playback notification tray showing the HudHud FM playback controls (monochrome vector play button, track title, and progress).

Surrounding the phone is a protective translucent glass shield radiating luminous acoustic soundwaves that flow effortlessly around everyday life obstacles (representing multitasking, screen locking, and app switching). The Hoopoe mascot is seen blissfully enjoying the music with eyes closed and hands resting on headphones, demonstrating effortless, peaceful listening.
