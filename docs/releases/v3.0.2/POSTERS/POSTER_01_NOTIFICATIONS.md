# Poster Concept 01 — Interactive Notifications & Live Alerts

* **Concept**: 3D Dark Acoustic Glassmorphism with Floating Notification Banner
* **Core Slogan**: «لحظة بلحظة... جديد إذاعاتك يصلك أولاً» (Every Moment... Your Radio Updates First)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: Floating smartphone with a glowing, rich media lock-screen push notification and the Hoopoe mascot

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A premium, cinematic 3D visual featuring a sleek smartphone floating slightly angled in the center of an intimate, dark acoustic broadcast studio. The phone screen illuminates the dark environment, displaying an elegant iOS/Android style lock screen with a vivid HudHud FM notification banner. The banner features an image of a cultural radio show, crisp Arabic text, and a glowing play icon.

Beside the phone, the friendly HudHud Hoopoe mascot wears professional studio headphones, holding a notification beacon or bell that emits soft golden sound particles. Swirling acoustic ribbons in rich burgundy (`#8B2648`) and warm amber connect the phone to the background studio environment.
