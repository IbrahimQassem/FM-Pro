# Poster Concept 03 — Terrestrial FM & Heritage Radio

* **Concept**: Classic Radio Tower Meets Digital Discovery
* **Core Slogan**: «من الأثير الأرضي إلى الفضاء الرقمي» (From Terrestrial Airwaves to the Digital Sphere)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: Traditional FM broadcast tower broadcasting waves that transform into digital streams inside the smartphone

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A poetic juxtaposition of classic radio heritage and modern digital streaming. On a distant hill, a traditional radio broadcast antenna tower stands silhouetted against a breathtaking Yemeni twilight sky (shades of deep purple, burgundy, and sunset amber). Concentric FM radio waves radiate from the antenna tower, and as they approach the foreground, they gracefully transform into crystalline digital audio waveforms entering a floating smartphone running HudHud FM.

The Hoopoe mascot stands atop a sleek acoustic console, looking through binoculars or tuning an antique brass dial that morphs into a sleek mobile UI dial, celebrating the preservation of local radio heritage in the modern digital era.
