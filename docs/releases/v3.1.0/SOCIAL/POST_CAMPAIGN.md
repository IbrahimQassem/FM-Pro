# Launch Campaign Schedule & Multi-Phase Strategy — v3.1.0

## Phase 1: Launch Day (اليوم الأول — إعلان الإطلاق)
* **Objective**: Announce the availability of v3.1.0 and drive app updates.
* **Key Asset**: Poster 01 (Station Social Connectivity).
* **Channels**: Push Notification (FCM Rich Feature), X/Twitter, Facebook, Telegram, WhatsApp Channel.

## Phase 2: Engagement & Social Wave (الأسبوع الأول — تفاعل المستمعين)
* **Objective**: Encourage listeners to explore their favorite station's social channels and WhatsApp hotlines.
* **Key Asset**: Video snippet / Carousel highlighting the station details view action chips.
* **Channels**: Instagram Stories & Reels, Facebook Community Posts.

## Phase 3: B2B & Broadcast Stations Focus (الأسبوع الثاني — لشركاء الإذاعة ومدراء المحطات)
* **Objective**: Present the Web Admin capabilities, technical telemetry, and Excel export to station managers and partners.
* **Key Asset**: Poster 02 (Super Admin Excel Export) & Poster 03 (Broadcast Engineering Specs).
* **Channels**: LinkedIn, Direct Station Partnership Proposals, Email Bulletins.
