## 1. Visual Composition & Elements
* **Background**: Studio Obsidian (`#140F12`) to Acoustic Burgundy (`#451222`) radial gradient with warm atmospheric golden light dust.
* **Hero Object**: Modern flagship smartphone displaying the HudHud FM Station Details screen with the new «روابط التواصل والموقع» card.
* **Surrounding Floating Elements**:
  - Glowing 3D glass icon orbs for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X/Twitter, WhatsApp, and Web Globe.
  - The HudHud Hoopoe Mascot with headphones, welcoming listeners with a warm gesture toward the social chips.
  - Golden soundwaves and dynamic equalizer lines radiating from the station artwork.
