# Poster Concept 01 — Live Radio & Acoustic Studio

* **Concept**: 3D Dark Studio Glassmorphism with Live Presenter & Mascot
* **Core Slogan**: «استمع. اكتشف. شارك. | هدهد إف إم 3.0» (Listen. Discover. Share.)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: Yemeni Radio Host alongside the official HudHud Hoopoe Mascot in an intimate high-tech broadcast studio

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A premium, cinematic 3D visual featuring a smiling Yemeni radio presenter seated at an illuminated dark frosted glass broadcast mixing console. To his side, the official HudHud Hoopoe mascot stands proudly wearing his signature dark plum hoodie adorned with golden ancient Musnad script and gold-trimmed studio headphones, holding a golden vintage microphone.

Between them, a floating flagship smartphone displays the live HudHud FM station playback interface (`Now Playing` with live frequency `95.0 FM`, glowing «مباشر (LIVE)» badge, and golden audio frequency spectrum). Above the console, an illuminated warm neon box sign reads **"ON AIR"**. Glowing golden sinusoidal soundwaves ripple across the studio space.

Anchoring the top right is the official embossed 3D HudHud logo with ancient Musnad inscription and crown crest feathers. The base of the poster is grounded by a sleek dark glass banner displaying the official Arabic slogan:
**«استمع. اكتشف. شارك | هدهد إف إم 3.0»**
