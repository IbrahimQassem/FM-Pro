# Poster Concept 01 — Live Radio & Acoustic Studio

* **Concept**: 3D Dark Glassmorphism Studio
* **Core Slogan**: «استمع. اكتشف. شارك.» (Listen. Discover. Share.)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: Hoopoe Radio Host in an intimate, high-tech acoustic broadcast studio

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A premium, cinematic 3D visual featuring the official HudHud Hoopoe mascot sitting comfortably at a sleek dark frosted glass broadcaster's console. The mascot wears professional matte-burgundy and chrome studio headphones and speaks with a friendly, welcoming expression into a vintage condenser microphone.
In the mid-ground, a floating high-end smartphone displays the live HudHud FM station playback interface (`Now Playing` with live streaming frequency and waveform indicator). Above the console, a warm crimson glowing neon sign reads **"ON AIR"**. Glowing acoustic soundwaves radiate smoothly across the scene in rich burgundy and emerald tones (`#8B2648` and `#1A8F5A`).

The official HudHud FM logo is placed with prominent visual hierarchy in the upper corner.
