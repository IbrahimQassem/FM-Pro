# Poster Concept 02 — Connected Community & Contact Us Hub

* **Concept**: 3D Acoustic Atmosphere with Floating Communication Channels
* **Core Slogan**: «صوتك مسموع... تواصل مباشر مع أسرة هدهد FM» (Your Voice is Heard... Direct Connection with the HudHud FM Family)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: Smartphone displaying the Contact Us sheet with floating luminous icons for WhatsApp, Phone, and Social Channels

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A cinematic studio scene bathed in moody violet and warm burgundy lighting. In the center, a smartphone floats showcasing the elegant HudHud FM «تواصل معنا» (Contact Us) bottom sheet, complete with quick-action cards for direct WhatsApp messaging, telephone calls, and official social media communities.

Luminous 3D glass badges representing WhatsApp, Phone, and social links float around the phone with soft ambient halos. The Hoopoe mascot stands happily nearby, holding an antique golden studio microphone in one wing while gesturing warmly toward the community contact hub. Golden light sparkles and glowing soundwave ribbons float gracefully across the scene, symbolizing responsive communication between listeners and broadcasters.
