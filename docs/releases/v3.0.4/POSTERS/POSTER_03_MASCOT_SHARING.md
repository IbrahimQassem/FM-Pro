# Poster Concept 03 — Luxury Mascot Sharing & Universal Audio

* **Concept**: 3D Digital Acoustic Card with the Hoopoe Mascot & Dynamic Equalizer Waves
* **Core Slogan**: «شارك متعة الاستماع... هدهد FM معك أينما كنت» (Share the Joy of Listening... HudHud FM with You Wherever You Are)
* **Primary Visual Focus**: 3D Hoopoe Mascot presenting a rich social share card with golden equalizer waves

---

## 1. Visual Composition & Narrative

A festive, premium 3D composition celebrating the community spirit of Yemeni radio. The HudHud Hoopoe mascot takes center stage, rendered with ultra-fine feather detail and expressive warm eyes, wearing his iconic burgundy studio headphones. With wings outstretched, he presents an elegant floating glass card showing the HudHud FM sharing experience—complete with application icon, radio station artwork, and high-fidelity equalizer waves.

In the background, luminous dynamic equalizer bars pulse in shades of amber and rose gold, set against an acoustic backdrop. A floating smartphone nearby displays the smooth radio playback interface, demonstrating universal audio playback and easy one-tap guest login.
