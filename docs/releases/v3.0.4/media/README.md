## 2. Media Asset Details & Feature Mapping

### Poster 01 — In-App Updates Engine (`FEAT-IN-APP-UPDATE`)
* **Creative Concept**: 3D Dark Acoustic Glassmorphism with Floating In-App Update Modal
* **Arabic Slogan**: «دائماً على أحدث إصدار... بلمسة ذكية واحدة»
* **Key Visuals**: Floating flagship phone presenting the modern `v3.0.4` update dialog, glowing burgundy action button «تحديث الآن», and the Hoopoe mascot holding a golden sparkle.
* **Specification Document**: [`../POSTERS/POSTER_01_IN_APP_UPDATES.md`](file:///Users/iq/AndroidStudioProjects/accelerate/hudhud_fm/docs/releases/v3.0.4/POSTERS/POSTER_01_IN_APP_UPDATES.md)

### Poster 02 — Contact Us Support Hub (`FEAT-CONTACT-HUB`)
* **Creative Concept**: 3D Acoustic Atmosphere with Floating Communication Channels
* **Arabic Slogan**: «صوتك مسموع... تواصل مباشر مع أسرة هدهد FM»
* **Key Visuals**: Floating smartphone displaying the «تواصل معنا» bottom sheet, surrounded by glowing 3D glass badges for WhatsApp, Phone hotline, and Social communities, with the Hoopoe mascot holding a vintage golden broadcast microphone.
* **Specification Document**: [`../POSTERS/POSTER_02_CONTACT_HUB.md`](file:///Users/iq/AndroidStudioProjects/accelerate/hudhud_fm/docs/releases/v3.0.4/POSTERS/POSTER_02_CONTACT_HUB.md)

* **Arabic Slogan**: «شارك متعة الاستماع... هدهد FM معك أينما كنت»
